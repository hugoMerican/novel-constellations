Novel Constellations

Novel Constellations is my love letter to books. Every book, whether my experience with it was good or bad, has left an impression. I feel that in some way, everyone knows that the content they surround themselves in and embrace have shaped themselves, however it was only upon creation of this site that I realised how much of who I am has been molded by literature. At the risk of sounding pretentious, books go beyond shaping our view of politics, creative leanings and idealogies, I feel books instill themselves as a core part of our being.
